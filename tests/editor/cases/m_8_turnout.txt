# Editing on the model (E4b): a switch dragged on a line of the model is a turnout of the catalogue inserted into the line, its switch
# track made by the generator, one step; Ctrl+Z takes it back with its tracks, Ctrl+Y brings it again. Moved along the line, given another
# type, the other side, or taken out by Del: a command of the model each, one step. One with a cant ramp inside it is refused, the tool
# telling why, and nothing changes. The empty ground east of k0..k3 (x 800..1300)
wait 5
modelediting 1
layclicks 0 600 800 -300 800 100
wait 2
expect modellines 1
expect steps 1
# dragged on the straight as the mouse drags it: 100 m from its start, the diverging track to -x
curvedswitch l1_se1 800 -200 797 -180 Rz_60E1-300-1:9
wait 2
modeltracks
expect modelrefused 0
expect modellines 4
expect steps 2
expect managed t1 any
expect switchpanel placed
expect modelends
undo
wait 2
expect modellines 1
expect steps 1
expect track t1 removed
expect modelends
redo
wait 2
expect modellines 4
expect track t1 present
expect modelends
# moved 20 m on along its line, given another type, the other side: a step each
switchslide t1 20
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect steps 3
expect modelends
switchreplace t1 Rz_60E1-500-1:12
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect steps 4
expect modelends
modelturnoutside t1
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect steps 5
expect modelends
modeltracks
# taken out by Del, once the scenarios of the map are looked through (none names it): the line one line again
modeldel t1
modelwait
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect steps 6
expect track t1 removed
expect modellines 1
expect modelends
# a line with a curve, its transition and cant ramp from 22 m of its start to 122 m, its arc on from there: a turnout bent into the arc
# 2 m past the end of the ramp is refused (the ramp ends 6 m off a turnout at least, ST-T1-A6 9.6 pkt 16), nothing changes
layclicks 0 600 900 -300 900 100 1200 400
wait 2
modeltracks
modelmark curve
switchcurve Rz_60E1-300-1:9 902.2 -176 1 1
wait 2
expect modelrefused 1
expect modelsame curve
expect steps 7
expect status 9.6 pkt 16
# laid again and saved: the switch track written as the generator made it and recorded, found so after the scenery is opened again
switchplace Rz_60E1-300-1:9 800 -200 1 1
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect managed t2 any
save
wait 5
expect handlines 0
quit
#> reopen
wait 5
modelediting 1
modeltracks
expect managed t2 any
expect modelends
expect handlines 0
quit
