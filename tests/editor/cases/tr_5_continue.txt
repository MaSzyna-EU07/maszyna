# Laying from the photo: after Enter the tool stays at the end laid, the next clicks carry the line on from there, joined to it, with no
# click on the free end; Esc ends it. The empty ground north-east of the fixture
wait 5
tap f3
wait 2
tap l
wait 2
laymode traced
laysigma 0.15
layp 2000 6000 2150 6000 2300 6000 2450 6000
wait 2
layenter
wait 2
expect laycontinue 1
expect laypanel laid shown
expect laid curves 0
# on along the same straight, then into a curve to the left
layp 2600 6000 2750 6000 2900 6000 3050 6002.5 3200 6012 3340 6030
wait 2
expect laypanel clicking folded
layenter
wait 2
expect laid joined 1
expect laid curves 1
expect laycontinue 1
expect steps 2
tap esc
wait 2
expect laycontinue 0
expect laypanel ready shown
quit
