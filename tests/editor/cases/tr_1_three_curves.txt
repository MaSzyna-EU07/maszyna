# Laying from the photo: a line of 3.2 km with three curves of the norms (R 1200, 900 the other way, 1500) clicked along the middle of the
# track as on the photo, sparsely, 20 clicks at most, in the shape traced on the photo, with no Tab and one Enter: the three curves are
# recognised, their radii within 3 %, laid at the speed of the line. The empty ground north-east of the fixture (x 2000.., z 2000..)
wait 5
tap f3
wait 2
tap l
wait 2
laymode traced
# from the photo the heights are fitted to the ground by themselves, nothing chosen in this run
expect layheights fitted
laysigma 0.2
laysample 2000 2000 0 500 300 160 0.15 30 1200 1130 -40 900 1130 25 1500 600
wait 2
expect laypanel clicking folded
layenter
wait 2
expect laypanel laid shown
expect laid clicks 20
expect laid curves 3
expect laid radius 0 1200 3
expect laid radius 1 900 3
expect laid radius 2 1500 3
expect laid speed 0 100
expect laid speed 1 100
expect laid speed 2 100
expect laykeys 0 1
quit
