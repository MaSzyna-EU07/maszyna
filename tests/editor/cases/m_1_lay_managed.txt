# Editing on the model (E4a): a line laid with the lay tool in the straights is a line of the model, its tracks made by the generator
# and marked as its, one step of the history; the project file is written next to the copy of the scenery when the map is opened and
# with the scenery; the ends of the tracks are the numbers of the nodes, to the bit, before the save and after the scenery is opened
# again, and nothing is taken for a change by hand. The old tracks stay old. The empty ground east of k0..k3 (x 800..1100)
wait 5
modelediting 1
expect projectfile present
expect projectfile lines 0
expect modellines 0
layclicks 0 600 800 -300 800 100 1100 400
wait 2
modeltracks
expect modellines 1
expect managed @1.0 1
expect managed @1.2 1
expect managed a1 none
expect modelends
expect steps 1
expect hand 1 0
save
wait 5
expect projectfile lines 1
quit
#> reopen
wait 5
modelediting 1
modeltracks
expect modellines 1
expect managed @1.0 1
expect modelends
expect hand 1 0
quit
