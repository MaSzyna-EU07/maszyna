# Editing on the model (E4a): split, join and delete of the lines of the model from the route design, each a command of the model and a
# step of the history. Two straights on one line apart (x 800, z -300..0 and 300..600) joined into one with a straight between them;
# the one in the middle split and deleted
wait 5
modelediting 1
layclicks 0 600 800 -300 800 0
wait 2
layclicks 0 600 800 300 800 600
wait 2
expect modellines 2
modelplan @1.0
modeljoin 800 0 800 300
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect modellines 3
expect managed @3.0 3
expect modelends
expect steps 3
modelplan @3.0
modelsplit 150
wait 2
expect modellines 4
expect modelends
modelplan @3.0
modeldelete
wait 2
expect modellines 3
expect steps 5
undo
wait 2
expect modellines 4
expect modelends
quit
