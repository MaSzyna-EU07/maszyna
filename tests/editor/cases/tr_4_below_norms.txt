# Laying from the photo: a curve of R 300 m on a line of 120 km/h, sharper than the norms allow at that speed, is laid slowed down to the
# speed its radius allows, 70 km/h, its tracks given that speed, with no question. One of R 150 m, below the least radius of a main track
# (PLK 180 m), isn't laid at all: the points stay to be put right. The empty ground north-east of the fixture
wait 5
setup norms plk speed 120 traffic mixed track main gauge 1435 rail 60E1
expect layspeed 120
tap f3
wait 2
tap l
wait 2
laymode traced
laysigma 0.15
laysample 2000 5000 0 300 150 25 0.1 50 300 300
wait 2
layenter
wait 2
expect laid curves 1
expect laid radius 0 300 3
expect laid speed 0 70
expect laid slowtracks 70 2
laycancel
laysample 3500 5000 0 300 150 15 0.1 60 150 300
wait 2
expect steps 1
layenter
wait 2
expect steps 1
expect laypanel clicking folded
quit
