# First steps: while the pointer is over a panel, the line the lay tool shows stays as it was: no leg follows the pointer under
# the panel to where it points through it. Back over the view, the line follows the pointer again. The points go on the empty
# ground between the turntable tt1 and the curves (x 220..270), the camera straight above them
wait 5
tap f3
wait 2
tap l
wait 2
camera 245 200 150 245 0 150.5
wait 3
layp 225 120 235 165
mouseat 255 0 175
wait 3
expect laycursor 255 175 0.5
# over the tool options at the left
mouse 40 200
wait 3
expect laycursor 255 175 0.5
# back over the view
mouseat 250 0 135
wait 3
expect laycursor 250 135 0.5
quit
