# Editing on the model (E4b): the grade line of a line of the model drawn in the profile tool is its command (set_grades), one step; a
# curve added to the line in the route design and taken out again, a command each. The empty ground east of k0..k3 (x 800..1100)
wait 5
modelediting 1
layclicks 0 600 800 -300 800 100
wait 2
expect modellines 1
expect steps 1
# a break 1 m up at 200 m of its 400 m, rounded by a vertical curve of R 10000 m
modelgrade @1.0 200 1 10000
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect modelgrades 1 1
expect steps 2
expect modelends
undo
wait 2
expect modelgrades 1 0
expect steps 1
redo
wait 2
expect modelgrades 1 1
expect modelends
# a break so near the end its vertical curve can't be laid there: refused by the model, nothing changes
modelmark graded
modelgrade @1.0 399.5 0.5 10000
wait 2
expect modelrefused 1
expect modelsame graded
expect steps 2
quit
