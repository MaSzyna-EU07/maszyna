# Editing on the model (E4a): the route design applied to old tracks makes them a line of the model in their place, one step, the
# tracks made by the model marked as its.
# Ctrl+Z brings the old tracks back. The straight a1, a2, a3 along x 0, z 0..300
wait 5
modelediting 1
route 0 150 0
routeapply
wait 2
expect modellines 1
expect managed @1.0 1
expect track a2 removed
expect modelends
expect steps 1
undo
wait 2
expect modellines 0
expect track a2 present
expect managed a2 none
quit
