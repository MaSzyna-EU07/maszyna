# stage 6: a road across two tracks of different cant (within the 119 mm a crossing allows) is laid into the plane of the heads of each,
# cut along the rails; the cant of a track changed, the road follows it; one undo takes the change of the cant and the road with it.
# p2 (x 120) and q2 (x 124.5): straights side by side along z from 100 to 200, the points of the tracks at 0, the heads 0.18 over them
wait 5
expect roadpieces 0
trackcant q2 0 35 35
wait 3
expect cant q2 start 35 0.5
# the road across both at z 150, laid at the height of the points, 0.18 under the heads; d2 (x 80) left out
roadlay 85 150 158 150 0.0 1 3.5
wait 10
crossingprobe
expect crossing holds 35 mm
# the surface at the middle of each head, where the road crosses it (50 m along each track)
expect railhead p2 left 50 0.001
expect railhead p2 right 50 0.001
expect railhead q2 left 50 0.001
expect railhead q2 right 50 0.001
# the gaps: the flangeway and the head of each rail (the right rail of a track along z lies at -x), none between the rails
expect roadgaps 119.22 150 1
expect roadgaps 120.78 150 1
expect roadgaps 120 150 0
expect roadgaps 123.78 150 1
expect roadgaps 125.22 150 1
expect roadgaps 124.5 150 0
expect roadgaps 122.25 150 0
# the road more than 30 m from the rails goes on as it was, at the height it was laid at
expect roadsurface 87 150 0.0 0.001
expect roadsurface 157 150 0.0 0.001
# the cant of q2 changed: the road over it follows
trackcant q2 0 20 20
wait 10
expect crossing holds 20 mm
expect railhead q2 left 50 0.001
expect railhead q2 right 50 0.001
expect railhead p2 left 50 0.001
expect roadgaps 123.78 150 1
# one undo: the cant of 35 mm and the road over it
undo
wait 5
expect cant q2 start 35 0.5
expect railhead q2 left 50 0.001
expect railhead q2 right 50 0.001
expect roadgaps 123.78 150 1
# and one more: the road and its crossing go together
undo
wait 5
expect roadpieces 0
quit
