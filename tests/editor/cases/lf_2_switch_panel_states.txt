# Laying flow: the panel of the switch tool goes through its states, each with its step at its top. A plain track selected: ready. A
# switch refused leaves it ready, the refusal told. A switch laid: placed, told so until another step of the history. Another switch
# laid after it and the first one selected again: selected. i0: a straight taking the cant of 60 mm on along all of it (a switch there
# breaks 11.4 pkt 7); a2 and s2: plain straights of the lines a and s, x 0 and x 40, z 100..200
wait 5
curvedswitch i0 300 -40 302 -20 Rz_60E1-300-1:9
expect switchpanel ready
expect status 11.4 pkt 7
curvedswitch a2 0 140 3 160 Rz_60E1-300-1:9
expect track z1 present
expect switchpanel placed
curvedswitch s2 40 140 43 160 Rz_60E1-300-1:9
expect track z2 present
expect switchpanel placed
select z1
wait 2
expect switchpanel selected
quit
