# the axis of the track), the road laid at the height of the heads (it ends 2 m past the track: an approach to an end lower down
# would be steeper than 3%): the road stays in the plane of the heads under them, cut along the rails, and leaves its surface out under
# their outlines, so its lanes, which the vehicles go along, stay at the top of the panels; the panels moved off the crossing, the
