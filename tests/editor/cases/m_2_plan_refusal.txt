# Editing on the model (E4a): a curve of a line of the model changed in the route design goes through the commands of the model. One
# the norms refuse (a cant of 200 mm) changes nothing, the scene and the model as they were, no step; the reason is told with its value,
# its limit and its paragraph. One they let through is laid, one step, the line's tracks made anew
wait 5
modelediting 1
layclicks 0 600 800 -300 800 100 1100 400
wait 2
expect modellines 1
modelmark laid
modelplan @1.0
routecant 0 200
routeapply
wait 2
expect modelrefused 1
expect modelmessage holds cant 200
expect modelmessage holds the limit
expect modelsame laid
expect steps 1
expect modelends
# the design read from the model again, the curve made wider
modelplan @1.0
expect modelvertex 1 0 radius 791
routeradius 0 820
routeapply
wait 2
expect modelrefused 0
expect modelvertex 1 0 radius 820
expect steps 2
expect managed @1.0 1
expect modelends
quit
