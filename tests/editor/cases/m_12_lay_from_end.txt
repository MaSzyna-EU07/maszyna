# Editing on the model (E4b): a line laid with the lay tool from the free end of a line of the model goes on from the node of that end,
# shared by both lines, not a free node of its own: the tracks of both end at the numbers of the node to the bit, before the save and
# after the scenery is opened again. The empty ground east of k0..k3 (x 800..1100)
wait 5
modelediting 1
layclicks 0 600 800 -300 800 100
wait 2
expect modellines 1
expect modelnodes 2
# on from its end at 800 100, northward
layclicks 0 600 800 100 800 400
wait 2
modeltracks
expect modelrefused 0
expect modellines 2
expect modelnodes 3
expect managed @2.0 2
expect modelends
expect steps 2
save
wait 5
quit
#> reopen
wait 5
modelediting 1
modeltracks
expect modellines 2
expect modelnodes 3
expect modelends
expect hand 1 0
expect hand 2 0
quit
