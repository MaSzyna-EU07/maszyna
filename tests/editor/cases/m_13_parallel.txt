# Editing on the model (E4b): the parallel tool beside a line of the model lays a line of the model at the spacing: its straights offset,
# its arc concentric with the line's, the radius less on the inside of the curve, the transitions recomputed, the spacing in the curve
# widened by the widening of the gauge of both tracks (none at this radius); checked by the norms, one step. The empty ground east of k0..k3 (x 800..1300)
wait 5
modelediting 1
layclicks 0 600 800 -300 800 100 1100 400
wait 2
expect modellines 1
expect steps 1
modelselect @1.0
# on the inside of the curve, which turns to +x: to the right of the line as it runs
parallel 4.5 -1
wait 2
modeltracks
expect modelrefused 0
expect modellines 2
expect managed @2.0 2
expect modelends
expect steps 2
# 4.5 m on the straight and in the arc, concentric (no widening of the gauge in a curve of R 250 m or more)
expect modelspacing 1 2 10 4.4999 4.5001
expect modelspacing 1 2 400 4.498 4.502
undo
wait 2
expect modellines 1
expect steps 1
quit
